Improve enterprise data protection and recovery readiness with Cohesity

BackupSteward provides vendor-independent backup and recovery consulting for organisations operating Cohesity. Engagements can focus on an operational issue, a health assessment, infrastructure changes or a review of recovery readiness. Scope and deliverables are agreed before work begins.

Cohesity Environment Assessment

Review platform health, protected workload coverage, configuration, policy alignment, capacity indicators and operational practices. Identify risks and prioritise practical improvements.

Protection Policy & Retention Review

Review protection policies, schedules, retention settings and workload assignments to identify potential protection gaps or configuration inconsistencies.

Backup Job Troubleshooting

Investigate recurring backup failures, workload connectivity, resource availability, performance symptoms and other operational issues using agreed logs and evidence.

Storage & Capacity Review

Review storage utilisation, capacity trends, performance indicators and platform health to identify potential constraints and improvement opportunities.

Immutability & Cyber Resilience

Review immutability and access-control considerations, retention requirements and recovery procedures as part of a wider ransomware resilience assessment.

Restore Testing & Recovery Readiness

Review restore procedures and available test evidence for files, virtual machines, databases and other protected workloads. Identify dependencies and validation gaps.

What a Cohesity review can cover

The review is tailored to your environment and may include the following areas, depending on platform version, architecture and access available.

  • Cohesity cluster and platform health indicators
  • Protection policies, schedules and retention
  • Protected workload coverage and exceptions
  • Storage utilisation, capacity and performance
  • Access controls and immutability considerations
  • Virtual, physical, NAS and cloud workload protection
  • Restore testing and recovery procedures
  • Operational documentation and remediation priorities

Typical engagement outcomes

  • A summary of the environment and agreed review scope
  • Documented observations supported by available evidence
  • Prioritised risks and improvement opportunities
  • Recommended troubleshooting or remediation steps
  • Restore-readiness observations and validation actions
  • A concise report or action plan, as agreed

A straightforward consulting process

A defined process keeps the work focused and makes the expected outcome clear.

1. Discover

Discuss your environment, issue, business impact and desired outcome.

2. Scope

Agree systems, evidence, access, deliverables, assumptions and timeline.

3. Assess

Review the agreed information, investigate symptoms and document findings.

4. Recommend

Provide prioritised next steps and validation guidance, with follow-up if agreed.

Frequently asked questions

Can you help troubleshoot Cohesity backup issues?

Yes. A scoped troubleshooting engagement can review job status information, logs, policy settings, workload connectivity and related infrastructure evidence to help identify likely causes and next steps.

Can you review an existing Cohesity environment?

Yes. A health assessment can review agreed areas such as architecture, policies, workload coverage, storage, operational practices and recovery readiness.

Do you support restore and disaster recovery reviews?

Yes. The review can examine available restore-test evidence, recovery procedures, dependencies and documentation, then identify gaps and recommended validation actions.

Do you work with other backup platforms?

Yes. BackupSteward also covers Commvault, Veritas NetBackup, Dell EMC NetWorker, Veeam and Rubrik, alongside additional enterprise data protection technologies.

Can consulting be delivered remotely?

Remote engagements can be considered where access, security requirements, working arrangements and scope are agreed in advance.

What information should I provide first?

Share the platform version if known, a high-level description of the issue, affected workloads, business impact and desired outcome. Do not send passwords, credentials or sensitive production data.
