Improve Commvault operations and recovery confidence

BackupSteward provides vendor-independent backup and recovery consulting for organisations operating Commvault environments. Engagements can focus on operational troubleshooting, environment assessments, infrastructure changes, cloud workloads or recovery readiness. Scope and deliverables are agreed before work begins.

Commvault Environment Assessment

Review CommCell architecture, infrastructure health, workload protection, configuration, capacity indicators and operational practices. Identify risks and prioritise improvements.

MediaAgent Troubleshooting

Investigate MediaAgent connectivity, communication, resource utilisation, performance symptoms and recurring operational issues using agreed logs and evidence.

Deduplication Database Review

Review DDB health, storage considerations, space utilisation, sealing and maintenance practices, and migration planning for deduplication databases.

Backup Job Troubleshooting

Investigate recurring backup failures, job delays, workload connectivity, scheduling, resource constraints and other operational symptoms.

Cloud & MediaAgent Migrations

Plan infrastructure transitions involving Windows and Linux MediaAgents, cloud deployments, storage changes and on-premises to cloud workload protection.

Restore Testing & Recovery Readiness

Review recovery procedures and test evidence for files, virtual machines, databases and other protected workloads. Identify dependencies and validation gaps.

What a Commvault review can cover

The review is tailored to your environment and may include the following areas, depending on platform version, architecture and access available.

  • CommServe, MediaAgent and client architecture
  • MediaAgent connectivity and communication
  • Backup job health, failures and scheduling
  • Deduplication database health and maintenance
  • Storage policies, schedules and retention
  • Disk libraries, cloud libraries and storage targets
  • Windows and Linux MediaAgent operations
  • VMware and Hyper-V workload protection
  • Oracle Cloud Infrastructure and hybrid environments
  • Content indexing and search considerations
  • Immutable backup and ransomware resilience
  • Restore testing, BMR and disaster recovery readiness

Typical engagement outcomes

  • Summary of the environment and agreed review scope
  • Documented technical observations supported by available evidence
  • Prioritised risks and improvement opportunities
  • Recommended troubleshooting and remediation steps
  • Migration considerations and validation checkpoints
  • Restore-readiness observations and recovery actions
  • A concise report or action plan, as agreed

Frequently asked questions

Can you help troubleshoot Commvault backup failures?

Yes. A scoped troubleshooting engagement can review job details, logs, configuration, workload connectivity and related infrastructure evidence to identify likely causes and next steps.

Can you help with MediaAgent issues?

Yes. The review can cover connectivity, communication, performance symptoms, resource utilisation and operational considerations for Windows and Linux MediaAgents.

Do you support DDB-related reviews?

Yes. Advisory work can cover DDB health, storage considerations, maintenance practices and migration planning, subject to the environment and agreed scope.

Can you help with cloud migrations?

Cloud and infrastructure migration planning can be considered for supported workloads and architectures, including hybrid environments and OCI deployments.

Do you cover other backup platforms?

Yes. BackupSteward also covers Veritas NetBackup, Dell EMC NetWorker, Veeam, Rubrik and Cohesity, alongside additional enterprise data protection technologies.

Can consulting be delivered remotely?

Remote engagements can be considered where access, security requirements, working arrangements and scope are agreed in advance.
