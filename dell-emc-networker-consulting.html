Improve visibility and recovery readiness across your NetWorker environment

BackupSteward provides vendor-independent backup and recovery consulting for organisations operating Dell EMC NetWorker. Engagements can focus on an operational issue, a health assessment, infrastructure changes or a review of recovery readiness. Scope and deliverables are agreed before work begins.

NetWorker Health Assessment

Review the backup environment, server and storage node architecture, client coverage, policy configuration, job outcomes and operational practices. Identify risks and prioritise improvement actions.

Backup Job & Workflow Troubleshooting

Investigate recurring backup failures, workflow behaviour, client communication, resource availability, device errors and other operational symptoms using agreed logs and evidence.

Client & Policy Protection Review

Review client configuration, protection groups, schedules, retention settings and workload requirements to identify potential coverage gaps or inconsistencies.

Storage Node & Device Review

Review storage node configuration, devices, pools, disk and tape workflows, capacity indicators and performance symptoms within the agreed environment.

Restore Testing & Recovery Readiness

Review restore procedures and available test evidence for files, databases, virtual machines and other protected workloads. Identify dependencies and validation gaps.

Migration & Modernisation Planning

Assess infrastructure changes, platform transitions, workload moves and operational dependencies. Develop a practical plan with assumptions, risks and validation checkpoints.

What a NetWorker review can cover

The review is tailored to your environment and may include the following areas, depending on platform version, architecture and access available.

  • NetWorker server and storage node architecture
  • Client configuration, protection groups and schedules
  • Backup workflow and job status analysis
  • Devices, pools, disk and tape-related workflows
  • Retention, capacity and performance indicators
  • Workload coverage and backup-window considerations
  • Restore testing, recovery procedures and documentation
  • Operational handover, runbooks and remediation priorities

Typical engagement outcomes

  • A summary of the environment and agreed review scope
  • Documented observations supported by available evidence
  • Prioritised risks and improvement opportunities
  • Recommended troubleshooting or remediation steps
  • Restore-readiness observations and validation actions
  • A concise report or action plan, as agreed

A straightforward consulting process

A defined process keeps the work focused and makes the expected outcome clear.

1. Discover

Discuss your environment, issue, business impact and desired outcome.

2. Scope

Agree systems, evidence, access, deliverables, assumptions and timeline.

3. Assess

Review the agreed information, investigate symptoms and document findings.

4. Recommend

Provide prioritised next steps and validation guidance, with follow-up if agreed.

Frequently asked questions

Can you help troubleshoot NetWorker backup issues?

Yes. A scoped troubleshooting engagement can review job status information, logs, policy settings, workload connectivity and related infrastructure evidence to help identify likely causes and next steps.

Can you review an existing NetWorker environment?

Yes. A health assessment can review agreed areas such as architecture, policies, workload coverage, storage, operational practices and recovery readiness.

Do you support restore and disaster recovery reviews?

Yes. The review can examine available restore-test evidence, recovery procedures, dependencies and documentation, then identify gaps and recommended validation actions.

Do you work with other backup platforms?

Yes. BackupSteward also covers Commvault, Veritas NetBackup, Veeam, Rubrik and Cohesity, alongside additional enterprise data protection technologies.

Can consulting be delivered remotely?

Remote engagements can be considered where access, security requirements, working arrangements and scope are agreed in advance.

What information should I provide first?

Share the platform version if known, a high-level description of the issue, affected workloads, business impact and desired outcome. Do not send passwords, credentials or sensitive production data.
