A clear, evidence-led process

Every engagement is agreed in advance and shaped around your objectives, available evidence, operational constraints and desired outcomes.

1. Discover

Discuss the environment, the challenge, objectives and expected outcome.

2. Agree scope

Confirm deliverables, access requirements, assumptions, exclusions and timeline.

3. Assess

Review agreed evidence, investigate the issue and prioritise findings by impact.

4. Recommend

Provide practical next steps and validation guidance, with follow-up if agreed.

About BackupSteward

Experience focused on recovery outcomes

BackupSteward is an independent backup and recovery consulting brand focused on helping IT teams understand risks, resolve technical challenges and make recovery plans more actionable.

Our approach is practical: review the available evidence, explain why an issue matters, and document what to do next. Engagements can be a one-time assessment, a defined-scope troubleshooting assignment or ongoing advisory support.

With more than 15 years of experience in backup and recovery environments, the focus includes day-to-day operational health, recovery validation, infrastructure transitions and improvement planning.

Frequently asked questions

A few details to help you decide whether BackupSteward is a fit for your requirements.

Do you work remotely?

Remote consulting can be considered where the scope, access method, security requirements and working arrangements are agreed in advance.

Can you help with an existing backup issue?

Share a high-level description of the symptoms, impact and workload. The next step is to confirm the scope and whether the issue fits the available expertise.

Can you help with migrations and immutable backups?

These can be considered as defined-scope advisory engagements, subject to the environment, requirements, access and agreed deliverables.

What will I receive?

Deliverables depend on the agreed scope and may include findings, prioritised recommendations, troubleshooting notes, a remediation plan or recovery-validation checklist.

How do we get started?

Email a short summary of your requirements. Please do not include passwords, credentials, production secrets or confidential data in your first message.
