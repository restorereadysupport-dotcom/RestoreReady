Enterprise backup consulting services

We provide independent technical guidance for organisations that need to improve backup reliability, review existing infrastructure or prepare for infrastructure and platform changes. Engagements are tailored to the environment and business objectives.

Backup Architecture Assessment

Review backup architecture, infrastructure components, data flows, storage design, scalability and potential single points of failure. Identify improvement opportunities for on-premises, cloud and hybrid environments.

Backup Operations and Troubleshooting

Investigate recurring backup failures, job performance issues, communication problems, media agent health, storage capacity and operational bottlenecks.

Virtualisation Protection

Review protection strategies for VMware and Microsoft Hyper-V environments, including workload coverage, backup policies, recovery options and infrastructure dependencies.

Cloud Backup Environments

Assess backup infrastructure and protection approaches across cloud and hybrid environments. Review connectivity, performance, storage utilisation and operational considerations.

Restore and Recovery Validation

Review restore procedures and available test evidence. Identify recovery dependencies and recommend practical testing to improve confidence in recoverability.

Disaster Recovery Readiness

Review recovery documentation, dependencies, recovery priorities and DR exercise outcomes. Identify gaps that may affect recovery during an outage or major incident.

What does an enterprise backup assessment cover?

Infrastructure Health

Review backup servers, media agents, proxies, storage components, connectivity and infrastructure capacity.

Policy and Retention Review

Examine backup schedules, retention settings, workload coverage and alignment with business requirements.

Operational Monitoring

Review job success rates, recurring failures, alerting, reporting and operational processes.

Security and Resilience

Review relevant access controls, backup isolation, immutability and protection of backup infrastructure.

Frequently asked questions

Do you provide vendor-independent backup consulting?

Yes. BackupSteward focuses on backup and recovery outcomes across multiple platforms. Recommendations are based on the environment and agreed scope.

Can you assess an existing enterprise backup environment?

Yes. An assessment can review architecture, backup operations, protection policies, infrastructure health and recovery readiness. The exact scope is agreed before work begins.

Do you support cloud and hybrid environments?

Yes. Reviews can include on-premises, cloud and hybrid backup infrastructure, subject to the technologies and scope agreed.

Can you help with recurring backup failures?

Yes. Troubleshooting engagements can examine job failures, infrastructure health, communication, performance and operational processes.

Do you provide restore testing?

Restore validation can be included in an agreed engagement. Testing must be authorised and planned with the relevant application and infrastructure owners.

What information should I include in my enquiry?

Share your backup platform, a brief description of your environment and the challenge you want to address. Do not email passwords, credentials, tokens or sensitive production data.
