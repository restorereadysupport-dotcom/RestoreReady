Immutable Backup & Ransomware Resilience Consulting

Immutable backups can help protect backup copies from unauthorised changes or deletion. However, immutability is only one part of a resilient recovery strategy. BackupSteward helps IT teams and managed service providers review protection controls, identify gaps and improve recovery readiness.

Discuss an Immutable Backup Review

What does an immutable backup review cover?

A focused review examines how protected backup copies are configured and how your organisation would recover if production systems or administrative accounts were compromised. The scope is agreed according to your platform, available evidence and business needs.

Immutability and Retention

Review protection settings, retention periods and lock controls against your recovery and compliance requirements.

Access and Administration

Examine privileged access, administrative pathways and controls that could affect the ability to alter or delete backup copies.

Backup Coverage and Gaps

Review workload coverage, backup policies, job outcomes and exceptions that may leave critical data unprotected.

Restore Validation

Review restore-test evidence, recovery dependencies and procedures. Identify where additional testing would provide better assurance.

Isolation and Resilience

Assess relevant considerations for separating backup infrastructure, copies and credentials from production environments.

Prioritised Action Plan

Document findings, potential impact, recommended improvements and practical validation steps.

Frequently asked questions

Does immutable backup guarantee ransomware recovery?

No. Immutability can help protect backup copies, but recovery also depends on backup coverage, access security, retention, infrastructure dependencies and successful restore testing.

Can you review an existing backup environment?

Yes. A review can be scoped around your existing environment, objectives and the evidence available.

Do you work with managed service providers?

MSP engagements can be considered for defined-scope backup reviews and technical advisory support.

What should I include in my first enquiry?

Share your backup platform, a high-level description of the concern and the outcome you need. Do not email passwords, credentials, tokens or sensitive production data.
